ACCESSING THE SERVICES

1. To get the credentials for your release:

   export JWT_SECRET=$(kubectl get secret -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} {{ include "ai-services.fullname" . }}-global-secrets -o jsonpath='{.data.jwtSecret}' | base64 -d)

2. To access the services via port-forward:

   kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} svc/ai-personalization-service 8080:8080

3. To check the status of all deployments:

   kubectl get deployments -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }}

4. To view the logs of a specific service:

   kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} deployment/ai-personalization-service -f

SCALING

To manually scale a service:

  kubectl scale deployment/ai-personalization-service -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} --replicas=5

HorizontalPodAutoscaler (HPA) is enabled for services with hpa.enabled=true

UPGRADING

To upgrade the release:

  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} . -f values.yaml

ROLLBACK

To rollback to a previous version:

  helm rollback {{ .Release.Name }}

UNINSTALLING

To uninstall the chart:

  helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }}

  Note: Persistent Volume Claims (PVCs) will not be removed by default.
  To remove them, run:

  kubectl delete pvc -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "ai-services.name" . }}
