### Partner Program Comparison

| Program Type | Description | Revenue Model | Target Profile | Margin |
|--------------|-------------|---------------|----------------|--------|
| **White-Label Reseller** | Resell Gogidix platform under your brand | Revenue share or wholesale | Logistics companies, tech providers | 30-50% |
| **Technology Partner** | Integrate Gogidix into your platform | Integration fees + revenue share | SaaS platforms, marketplaces | Variable |
| **System Integrator** | Implement Gogidix for enterprise clients | Project fees + recurring | Consulting firms, SIs | 20-40% |
| **Referral Partner** | Refer clients to Gogidix | One-time commission | Agencies, advisors | 10-20% |
| **Marketplace Partner** | Sell on Gogidix marketplace | Transaction fees | Brands, manufacturers | Per transaction |
