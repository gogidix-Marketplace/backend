## 1. SCREEN DIMENSIONS & LAYOUT

### 1.1 Device Support

| Device Type | Screen Size | Layout Strategy |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Phone (Small) | 375x667 (iPhone SE) | Single column, compact |
| Phone (Regular) | 390x844 (iPhone 14) | Standard layout |
| Phone (Large) | 428x926 (iPhone 14 Pro Max) | Extended content |
